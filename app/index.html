How to Use the Downloader

  1. Paste a media URL or playlist URL into a queue row.
  2. Choose that URL's output:
    • Base: Download the original media. Playlists are supported.
    • H264: Re-encode videos for broad device compatibility. Each video is limited to 120 minutes.
    • GIF: Create silent GIFs. Each video is limited to 10 minutes.
    • MP3: Extract audio from videos or playlists.
    • Transcribe: Create timestamped written transcripts with a local Whisper model.
  3. Use Add another URL to give each item its own output choice, then click Download or Download queue.

Tools:

  • Click a media title to play it or a transcript title to read it.
  • Click the icon to refresh the downloads list.
  • Click the icon to delete a completed file.
  • Click the icon to save a completed file.

Tips:

  • The downloader supports YouTube, Vimeo, Facebook, X, Instagram, TikTok, DailyMotion, Twitch, Reddit, SoundCloud, and many other providers supported by yt-dlp.
  • Playlist items are numbered and processed in order. Up to 50 items are accepted from one playlist.
  • Transcription runs on this server's CPU, so long videos may take a few minutes.
